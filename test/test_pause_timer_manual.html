🔍 Pause Timer Manual Test

This test verifies that the session timer pauses when you switch tabs or minimize the browser window.

Test Instructions:

  1. Open the main app in another tab: index.html
  2. Navigate to the Study tab
  3. Click "Start Study Session" to begin a session
  4. Note the time when you start (check your watch/phone)
  5. Switch to a different tab or minimize the browser for exactly 10 seconds
  6. Return to the app tab
  7. Complete the session (mark all cards as Easy/Review/Bury)
  8. Check the session complete message

Expected Result:

The elapsed time shown in the session complete message should NOT include the 10 seconds you spent in another tab.

Example: If you were actively studying for 20 seconds and paused (in another tab) for 10 seconds, the message should show approximately 20 seconds, not 30 seconds.

💡 How it works:

  • The app uses the visibilitychange event to detect when you switch tabs
  • When the tab becomes hidden, it records the pause start time
  • When you return, it calculates the pause duration and subtracts it from the total time
  • Multiple pause/resume cycles are supported

✅ Success Criteria:

  • Time counter pauses when app tab is hidden
  • Time counter resumes when you return to the app tab
  • Final session time excludes all pause durations
  • Works correctly with multiple pause/resume cycles

Note: The pause feature only works during an active study session. It does not affect time when you're just browsing the app without an active session.

Additional Test Scenarios:

  • Multiple Pauses: Switch away multiple times during a session (e.g., 5s, 10s, 3s) and verify all pause times are excluded
  • Quick Switches: Rapidly switch tabs to ensure the timer handles quick transitions
  • End While Paused: Switch to another tab and click "End Session" from the notification/alert to ensure it handles this edge case
